6-Button Character

Legend
(LP = Light Punch) (MP = Medium Punch) (HP = Heavy Punch)
(LK = Light Kick) (MP = Medium Kick) (HK= Heavy Kick)

Example for key setups
(LP = x) (MP = y) (HP = z)
(LK = a) (MK = b) (HK = c)

[Basic standing moves]
Punches
-LP = Quick Jab (press again after the first punch hits for a second follow-up hit)
-MP = Shove (press when up close to the enemy for a powerful Rib Crusher)
-HP = Machete Massacre (press again after the first slice makes contact to execute a second hit)

Kicks
-LK = Clown Kick
-MK = Disgraceful Kick
-HK = Chainsaw Flurry (when the move finishes, Needles will discard the chainsaw; is a projectile)

[Basic crouching moves]
Punches
-LP = Crouching Chop
-MP = Uppercut (this move sets up good lead-ins for aerial combos)
-HP = Leg Slash (no combo-ability. Useful against small characters)

Kicks
-LK = Crouching Clown Kick (can hit downed enemies)
-MK = Ankle Breaker/Ground Stomp (slow start-up, but powerful. Can down, and hurt downed enemies)
-HK = Trip/Explosive Present ("Trip" is only active when you have less than 500 super meter)

[Basic Aerial Attacks]
Punches
-LP = Aerial Quick Punch
-MP = Aerial Overhand Slam
-HP = Aerial Machete Swipe

Kicks
-LK = Aerial Clown Kick
-MK = Chainsaw Copter (carries player upwards slightly. Excellent for aerial combos)
-HK = Aerial Stomp

[Special Moves - Ground]

-Down, Back, MP = Initiate Clown's Edge Combo (press "MP" again after hitting, then finish with "HP")
-Down, Forward, MP = Shotgun Blast!
-Down, Forward, HP = Go for a spin!
-Down, Back, HP = Criss Cross Combo 
-Back, Forward, HP = "Don't play with sharp objects!" (aka Machete Throw)
-Down, Forward, MK = Hellfire Breath
-Down, Back, MK = "Who wants a treat?"
-Down, Back, LP = Fire Missile!
-Down, Back, LK = Freeze Missile!
-Down, Forward, LK = SMG Spray'n'Pray
-LP while running = Dashing Grab
-HK while running = Dashing Clown Kick
-LP+LK = Shield Dash (can cancel into Dashing Clown Kick by pressing "HK")
-LP+MP while guarding at long range = "Cut it out!" Ranged counter attack
-LK+MK while guarding at close range = "This is my curse!" Counter attack grab 
-HK while crouch guarding = Quick trip counter

[Special Moves - Air]

-Back, Forward, HP = Air knife
-Down (hold), HK = Aerial Slam Down

[Hypers]

Level 1
-Down, Down Forward, Foward, LP+MP = TNT Tag
-Down, Down Forward, Forward, LP+LK = "Satisfy your Sweet Tooth!" (note: is only usable at 500 health or below)
-Down, Down Forward, Forward, LK+MK = Mega Gun Barrage

Level 2
-Down, Down Back, Back, LP+MP = Taste the Tooth! (note: is disabled during team play)
-Down, Down Back, Back, MP+HP = "Fear the Reaper!" (note: is disabled during team play)

Level 3
-Down, Down Forward, Forward, MP+HP = "A butcher like me..." (note: is disabled during team play)
-Down, Down Back, Back, LP+LK = "Welcome to Twisted Metal" (note: can only be used up-close; diabled for team play)

[Taunts]
-Start (or "Enter" key) = Taunt
-Down+Start = Dancing Taunt


--------------
[Special Attribute]
Choosing Sweet Tooth's 5th and 6th palette will activate Dark Tooth and Gold Tooth mode, respectively. Dark Tooth
offers a significant attack power boost while Gold Tooth gives Sweet Tooth a defense buff.


Notice: "Chainsaw Flurry", "Aerial Overhand Slam", "Aerial Stomp", "Shove", the second hit of 
"Machete Massacre", "Aerial Machete Swipe", "Ankle Breaker", and "Disgraceful Kick" are all 
intended to be combo finsishers. As such, they cannot use follow-up moves after making contact.
Most everything else can work for combos though, so try to experiment and see what you can do!
